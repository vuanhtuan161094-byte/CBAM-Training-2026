## Legal accuracy
1. Fetch every applicable original text from EUR-Lex/Official Journal; verify amendments, date in force, Article, paragraph and Annex. Guidance is not binding law and legislative proposals are not current law.
2. Attach source ID, specific legal provision and URL to every legal and technical claim; mark unresolved claims NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW and do not silently assume.
3. Distinguish installation emissions, SEE direct/indirect, actual/verified/default precursor emissions, benchmark, free allocation factor, CSCF, carbon-price credit, cost scenario, importer exemption and annual obligations.
4. Never treat 97.5% of benchmark as 97.5% exemption from actual SEE. Do not double-count bought billet. Scrapped inputs and 2026 finishing rules must be checked from current operative legal text.
5. Separate original EU files from Vietnamese translations; preserve legal numbering, equations and tables. Every AI-assisted translation must say: BẢN DỊCH THAM KHẢO — CHỜ HIỆU ĐÍNH — KHÔNG PHẢI VĂN BẢN CHÍNH THỨC CỦA EU.
6. Do not call the 2024 transitional communication Excel a legally required form for 2026. Investigate current official operator reporting / Registry fields and label any CTIC-created template.
