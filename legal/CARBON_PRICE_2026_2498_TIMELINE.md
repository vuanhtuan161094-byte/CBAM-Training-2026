## Article 8 and Annex III — operator carbon-price report

- Article 8(1): the operator prepares the report using the method in Annex I and the Commission electronic template in the CBAM Registry; the report contains at least the Annex III information.
- Article 8(2): a registered operator sends it to the independent person through the Registry. An unregistered operator sends it by other appropriate means.
- Article 8(3): the report is prepared in English.
- Annex III includes operator/installation identifiers; report identifier/version and reporting period; identifiers of the verified operator's emissions report and its verification report; carbon-price mechanism and whether it covers direct or indirect emissions; legal basis; price and emissions data; and specified rebates/compensation.

## Role and report separation

| Function | Governing material | Working terminology |
|---|---|---|
| Emissions calculation and report | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Arts. 7–8; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547, Art. 10 / Annex IV | **Operator's emissions report** (OER); reviewed by a CBAM emissions **verifier** accredited for the relevant scope. |
| Carbon price effectively paid | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Art. 9; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2498, Arts. 8, 12–15 / Annex III | **Operator's carbon price report**; examined/certified by an **independent person** under the 2026/2498 rules. This is the legal term; do not replace it with “emissions verifier.” |

P0 did not verify a standalone public 2026 carbon-price report file. Article 8 specifies a Registry electronic template; this draft does not recreate it.
