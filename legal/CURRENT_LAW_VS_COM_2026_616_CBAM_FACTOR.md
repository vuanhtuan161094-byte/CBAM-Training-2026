# CBAM factor — current law compared with COM(2026) 616 proposal

**Research cut-off:** 17 November 2026. **Status:** research comparison; legal/human review pending. The proposal column is not law.

## Instruments and traceability

- **Current law:** Article 10a(1a) of Directive 2003/87/EC, inserted by Directive (EU) 2023/959, Annex I, point (13)(b). Official source: [`ACT-2023-959`](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/959/oj/eng), local OJ English SHA-256 `8c4b7922229bb8b735da1896f7cc599086f191ef20e7f95a6cc737eb9a6176e5`.

[Official proposal](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52026PC0616) · [procedure 2026/0212/COD](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/procedure/EN/2026_212).
- **Status at cut-off:** EUR-Lex lists the ordinary legislative procedure as **ongoing**. COM(2026) 616 has no operative effect at this cut-off. Proposal source is PAGE_ONLY in the P0/P1 register; no locally stored proposal PDF/hash is claimed.
- **Separate benchmark instruments:** the factor below concerns phase-out/reduction of EU ETS free allocation for CBAM goods. It is not a product benchmark value. CBAM free-allocation adjustment benchmarks are governed separately by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2620; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1412 revises EU ETS benchmarks for 2026–2030 and is not itself a CBAM benchmark amendment.

## Additional goods proposed for inclusion

COM(2026) 616, Article 1(15)(b)(ii), separately proposes a factor of 97.9 % in the first year and 95.8 % in the second year of the next five-year period following the additional goods’ inclusion in CBAM Annex I. It proposes a 100 % factor between the start of inclusion and that next five-year period, followed by the proposed schedule above. These rates are **proposal-only**, contingent on the proposed inclusion and legal adoption; do not treat them as current factors for existing goods.

## Interpretation controls

1. The CBAM factor reduces the amount of free allocation under the EU ETS for production of goods listed in CBAM Annex I. It is not a percentage exemption from actual specific embedded emissions (SEE), not a free allocation benchmark, and not a direct reduction in importer certificate surrender obligations.
2. Do not multiply or subtract these percentages from actual SEE. Keep the certificate free-allocation adjustment method in Regulation (EU) 2025/2620 separate from the phase-out schedule in Directive 2003/87/EC Article 10a(1a).
3. Do not import 2026/1412 EU ETS benchmark values into the CBAM free-allocation adjustment. The CBAM benchmark status for imports from 1 January 2027 remains **NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW** until a binding CBAM amendment is identified and checked against the OJ.
4. Proposal punctuation and number formatting follow the source text; the table normalizes numeric formatting for comparison only.
