# CBAM benchmark legal status for 2026–2027

**Cut-off:** 17 November 2026. **Status:** unresolved legal baseline; no benchmark number is approved or reproduced here.

## Two different instruments

| Layer | Official instrument | What it governs | Status at cut-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBAM free-allocation adjustment | Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2620 under CBAM Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Art. 31 | CBAM adjustment calculation and CBAM benchmark values in its own Annex | Applies from 1 January 2026. Recital 10 says 2026 benchmarks use estimated ETS values, should be reviewed after final ETS values are published, and updated CBAM values should apply to goods imported from 1 January 2027. |
| EU ETS free allocation | Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1412 under Directive 2003/87/EC, Art. 10a(2) | Revised ETS benchmarks for harmonised EU allocation from 2026 to 2030 | Published 29 June 2026; concerns ETS allocation. It does not itself amend CBAM Regulation 2025/2620. |
| COM(2026) 619 proposal | Proposal for a Regulation amending Directive 2003/87/EC on heat/fuel benchmarks | Proposed changes to ETS fallback-benchmark free-allocation levels for 2026–2030; the proposal says the resulting adjustment would be reflected through updated ETS benchmark values | Legislative procedure 2026/0211/COD remained ongoing at 17 November 2026. Proposal only; not operative law and not an amendment to CBAM act 2025/2620. |
| COM(2026) 616 proposal | Proposal amending Directive 2003/87/EC and Decision (EU) 2015/1814 | Broader ETS review. Its proposed Article 10a(1a) text also sets a separate factor for goods newly added to CBAM Annex I: 97.9% for the first year and 95.8% for the second year of the next five-year period, followed by the proposed schedule. | Legislative procedure 2026/0212/COD remained ongoing at 17 November 2026. Proposal only; not operative law and does not amend CBAM Regulation 2025/2620. Do not apply the proposed factors to current goods or the 2026 claim. |
| Expected CBAM update | A separate binding CBAM amending act, if adopted | Would amend the CBAM instrument and establish operative CBAM values | No binding CBAM amendment or replacement 2027 values was verified in official sources searched by 17 November 2026. The two ETS proposals above do not establish CBAM values. Treat the update as **not verified/adopted for this draft**, not as an assumed value set. |

## Source-aligned reading

1. Recital 10 of Regulation 2025/2620 says 2026 CBAM benchmarks use estimated ETS benchmarks and calls for review after final ETS benchmarks are published. It says updated CBAM benchmarks based on final ETS values should apply to goods imported from 1 January 2027. A recital describing intended review is not evidence that a later amendment was adopted.
2. Article 1 and the Annex of Regulation 2025/2620 contain CBAM adjustment rules and benchmark schedules. Use that act and any later binding CBAM amendment for CBAM calculations.
3. Article 1 and the Annex of Regulation 2026/1412 concern harmonised EU ETS free allocation under Directive 2003/87/EC. Its values must not be pasted into a CBAM calculation as though it amended the CBAM law.
4. The P0 Commission benchmark workbook is an informational aid, not the legal instrument. Compare it with current OJ law only after a human reviewer resolves the amendment status.
5. For a CN good and production route, a later model needs an exact trace from the CBAM good/category and precursor treatment to the operative CBAM Annex entry. This note makes no extrapolation to 2027 values.
6. COM(2026) 619 expressly concerns proposed EU ETS heat/fuel fallback benchmark changes; it may affect the ETS benchmark review context if adopted, but its proposed text is not current law and does not itself amend 2025/2620. COM(2026) 616 is a separate ongoing ETS-reform proposal that also proposes a distinct factor for additional goods newly added to CBAM Annex I (97.9% in year one and 95.8% in year two of the next five-year period). Neither proposal is evidence of a binding CBAM benchmark update or changes the operative 2026 factor for goods already covered.

## Release gate

- Search EUR-Lex/OJ for a binding act that explicitly amends 2025/2620 or replaces its CBAM Annex; save the OJ URL, date, amendment provisions, operative date and affected rows.
- If no act is located, have CTIC legal review decide how to explain recital 10 and which Annex values govern each import date.
- Do not substitute ETS figures, infer a 2027 value, or mark this issue resolved from a workbook.
- Record numeric benchmarks only after the legal review checkpoint.
