# EU CBAM applicability baseline for 2026

**Research cut-off:** 17 November 2026. **Status:** source-mapped working note for CTIC legal review; not legal advice and not CTIC legal approval. Read the Official Journal (OJ) text for legal effect. Consolidated EUR-Lex texts are documentation tools.

## How to use this inventory

- “In force” means an operative act was identified as at the cut-off; the date each provision applies may differ.
- Commission guidance, Registry manuals, spreadsheets and website summaries are not substitutes for legislation.
- An unresolved interpretation is labeled **NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW**. Do not turn an unresolved item into a course rule or numeric claim.
- Use source IDs from [`SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`](../source-register/SOURCE_REGISTER.csv). Downloaded official originals are under [`sources/official/originals`](../sources/official/originals/).

## Transitional and proposed sources

- Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773 and the May 2024 importer guide concern the transitional phase. Keep them as historical/context sources and label them `TRANSITIONAL_ONLY`.
- The 18 Dec 2024 installation communication template and its examples are transitional support materials. No evidence found that this Excel file is a required 2026 definitive-period form.
- COM(2025) 989 (downstream scope / anti-circumvention) and COM(2025) 990 (temporary decarbonisation fund) are proposals, not current operative law. Do not teach proposed changes as applicable obligations.

## Source and licensing controls

- The Commission's general reuse notice says its EU-owned web content is CC BY 4.0 unless an item states otherwise; third-party content needs separate rights clearance. See [European Commission legal notice](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en).
- Guidance No. 3, footnote 79, states that further use or redistribution of the IEA-licensed grid emission-factor data is permitted only for non-commercial purposes. The official default workbook and Guidance No. 3 PDF are linked in the register but not copied into this public repository pending an appropriate commercial licence/rights review.
- A link to an official page records discovery; only rows marked `RETRIEVED` with matching byte count and SHA-256 have a local original.
