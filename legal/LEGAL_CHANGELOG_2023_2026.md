# CBAM legal change log, 2023–2026

**Research cut-off:** 17 November 2026. **Status:** source-aligned working draft; all legal conclusions remain **NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW**. OJ texts are the legal source; consolidated EUR-Lex text is a documentation aid. This is not legal advice or CTIC approval.

## Proposal and transition controls

- COM(2025) 989 and COM(2025) 990 remain recorded in P0 as **proposals**. No proposal language is presented as current obligation. Recheck for enacted OJ instruments before later teaching.
- The 2024 installation communication template and examples remain **TRANSITIONAL_ONLY**. Definitive-period report requirements derive from the 2026-applicable legal acts and Registry.
- Annex I product boundaries, Annex II calculation details and all country/origin/precursor tests still need translation coverage. See [translation coverage](../translations/coverage.csv).
