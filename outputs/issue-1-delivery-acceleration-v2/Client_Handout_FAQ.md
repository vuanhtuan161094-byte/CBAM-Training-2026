# CTIC Vietnam CBAM 2026 — customer handout and FAQ

**INTERNAL DRAFT / NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION** · **NOT LEGAL ADVICE** · Training cut-off: 18 November 2026.

All workbook values are synthetic. Aluminium example: CN 7604 output and CN 7601 billet. No private supplier/client data, published defaults, verified benchmark, verified CSCF or forecast price is used.

## 30-day preparation checklist
1. Map importer, customs representative, CN codes, origin and calendar-year net mass by importer.
2. Map supplier installations, routes, goods, precursors, boundary and reporting period.
3. Request monitoring plan, emissions report, source activity data, balances, precursor evidence and correction history.
4. Confirm actual/default route, verifier scope/accreditation, Registry forms and submission timing.
5. Reconcile customs mass, supplier data, output, inventory and precursor use. Escalate legal/data gaps.

## FAQ

### Does 50 tonnes apply per product or shipment?
The single threshold aggregates covered CN codes per importer and calendar year. If exceeded, obligations apply to all covered imports in that year. Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Article 2a and Annex VII, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2025/2083.

### When is the 2026 annual declaration due?
By 30 September 2027 for calendar-year 2026. See Regulation (EU) 2023/956 Article 6(1), and Article 22 for surrender.

### Is the 2026 CBAM factor a 97.5% exemption from actual emissions?
No. It is an input to the free-allocation adjustment; it does not reduce actual SEE by 97.5%.

### Why are workbook benchmark and price inputs yellow?
They are synthetic. EU ETS benchmarks in Regulation (EU) 2026/1412 differ from CBAM BM under Regulation (EU) 2025/2620. Current CBAM BM values require review.

### Can the example be used for a real importer?
No. Replace every synthetic value with controlled data, verify operative provisions/defaults and Registry requirements, and obtain independent review. Workbook: NOT VERIFIED FOR EXTERNAL USE.
