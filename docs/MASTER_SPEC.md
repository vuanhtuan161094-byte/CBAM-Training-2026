# CTIC VIETNAM CBAM — Project brief

Baseline research date: 2026-11-17. Repository PUBLIC by owner choice; confidential records stay outside.

Deliverable A — Expert course: 2 days x 6h, 120–160 slides, Vietnamese technical lecture notes and handbook, legal register, case exercises with solutions, checklists, source-aligned unofficial Vietnamese translations.
Deliverable B — Client briefing: 150 minutes, 40–55 visually polished slides, handout, FAQ, concrete cost examples and prioritised business actions; no confidential business or client case data.

## Curriculum
EU legislative architecture, ETS relationship, applicability/CN classification, declarants and non-EU operators, 50-ton threshold, prices, certificates, annual dates, defaults, country carbon payments, benchmark, free allocation and EU ETS phase-out.
Deep steel: BF-BOF, EAF, DRI, scrap, ironmaking, ferroalloys, primary feedstock and downstream products.
Deep aluminium: smelting, casting, billets, extrusions, anodizing/finishing boundaries, scrap and recycled aluminium, purchased precursors, multi-route benchmark and data verification.
Deep cement: process calcination, clinker, fuels, cement blending and electricity, clinker precursors and factor.
Brief fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity.
Installation Monitoring Plan, source streams, calculation methods, mass balance, QA/QC, materiality, missing data and reporting.
Operator Emissions Report, Summary Report, relevant Excel templates, O3CI/CBAM Registry, verifier accreditation and verification stages, site visits, reports, uncertainty and readiness.
