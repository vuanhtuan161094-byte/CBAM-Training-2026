# Ten-claim EU legal citation audit

**Cut-off:** 17 November 2026. **Draft status:** primary-source audit seeds for CTIC review; not legal approval. Quotes below are intentionally short; source links point to the official EUR-Lex/OJ or Commission page.

| # | Claim under audit | Primary provision | Short source wording | Audit result / guardrail |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 2026 import year's first CBAM declaration is due in 2027. | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, consolidated 20 Oct 2025, Art. 6(1), `ACT-2023-956-CONS-2025-10-20` | “for the first time in 2027 for the year 2026” | **Supported.** Article 6(1) sets the 30 September annual deadline and Registry submission for the preceding calendar year. Recheck legislation before later release. |
| 2 | The single mass-based threshold is 50 tonnes net mass per calendar year, subject to the act's scope and exceptions. | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Arts. 2a and 2(3), Annex VII point 1; amendment by 2025/2083 | “50 tonnes of net mass” | **Supported with conditions.** Do not imply the threshold applies without exceptions or to electricity/hydrogen, which the current act treats specially. |
| 3 | Actual embedded emissions require verification under the CBAM verification framework. | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Art. 8 and Annex VI; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546; Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551 | “shall be verified” | **Supported.** Actual/default routes and specific categories have distinct rules; cite the applicable goods/method and the operative verification act. |
| 4 | The OER's content is governed by the definitive-period calculation act. | Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547, Art. 10 and Annex IV points 1.1, 1.1.1 and 1.2 | “operator’s emissions report” | **Supported.** Article 10 and Annex IV define required content and electricity addendum fields. No standalone public 2026 workbook was verified. |
| 5 | The verification report is an electronic Registry template, not a public template confirmed in this inventory. | Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546, Art. 6 and its Annex | “provided by the Commission via the CBAM Registry” | **Supported.** Law governs required content; this research did not find a public standalone file. Do not fabricate one. |
| 6 | Binding default values are in the legal act and its 2026 correction; the linked workbook is informational. | Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/2621 and 2026/1740; Commission legislation index; `ACT-2025-2621`, `ACT-2026-1740`, `WORKBOOK-DEFAULTS-2026` | Commission index: “for information purposes only” | **Supported.** Use Annex I/IV as corrected from 1 Jan 2026. | 7 | The 2026 ETS free-allocation factor is not a 97.5% exemption from actual imported emissions. | Directive 2003/87/EC, Art. 10a(1a) and Annex II, as amended by Directive (EU) 2023/959 | “97,5 % for 2026” | **Supported as a distinction.** 97.5% is part of the EU ETS free-allocation phase-out schedule used in the CBAM adjustment framework; it is not the percentage of actual embedded emissions exempted. |
| 8 | CBAM free-allocation adjustment uses a dedicated implementing act; the status of benchmark updates needs resolution. | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Art. 31; Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/2620 and (EU) 2026/1412 | “reviewed at the latest one month after” | **Partly resolved. | 9 | Carbon prices paid in a third country are addressed by basic Regulation Article 9 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2498; the latter is not yet in force at the cut-off. | Regulation (EU) 2023/956, Art. 9; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2498, Arts. 8 and 36 | “shall apply from 1 January 2026” | **Supported with temporal qualification.** OJ publication is 16 Nov 2026; Article 36 enters it into force on 19 Nov 2026 and provides application from 1 Jan 2026. Article 8 also specifies a Registry electronic template for the operator carbon-price report. |
| 10 | The 2024 installation communication Excel is transitional support, not established as a required 2026 legal form. | Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773 (transitional phase); Commission communication template dated 18 Dec 2024; definitive-period Regulations (EU) 2025/2546 and 2025/2547 | Commission page labels the guide template as a “CBAM communication template for installations” | **Supported with a scope inference.** The template belongs to transitional materials; no 2026 legal requirement to use this 2024 file was found. Label `TRANSITIONAL_ONLY`; use the definitive legal acts and Registry workflow for 2026. |
